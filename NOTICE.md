# Notice

Koyomi Calendars is MIT-licensed code (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

**Drawings.** Every drawing in this package is original: its own shapes, gradients and a stroke-drawn digit set. No image, font file, icon or logo from anyone else is used, and no design is named after a company or a product. The designs are inspired by looks that calendar pages have long had (a flip calendar, a tear-off pad, a spiral-bound wall page, a rounded tile) and copy none of them. No SPX, SPXN or SPXIS mark is in this package.

**Words.** The names of months and weekdays come from the `Intl` support of the reader's own browser or Node. The package ships no list of them. The Japanese labels and holiday names in the source are plain words of the language.

**Dates and the Japanese calendar.** All dates are computed. The public holidays follow the Act on National Holidays (国民の祝日に関する法律) as amended, for 2000 to 2099, with the equinox days from the formula the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan (国立天文台) publishes for 1980 to 2099. The old calendar (旧暦) is computed from Jean Meeus, *Astronomical Algorithms*, 2nd ed. (1998), chapters 25 and 49, for 1900 to 2100. Nothing is looked up in a table or downloaded, and the package holds no holiday data for any other country.

**Data.** The package holds no dataset and makes no network request.
